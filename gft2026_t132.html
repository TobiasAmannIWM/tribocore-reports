⓪ Talk Overview
GfT 2026 · Talk T132 · Session: Tribological Data Management & Digitalisation
Abstract

TriboCore KIDaT is a FAIR-ready tribology data pipeline at Fraunhofer IWM that processes raw instrument files from eight measurement methods into a queryable knowledge graph (Neo4j + Fuseki) with full provenance. Applied to DLC coatings (ta-C, a-C:H, a-C:H:Si) in the SupraSlide project, the pipeline extracts 52+ physics-based Kennwerte per B3P experiment — including EHL film thickness (Hamrock-Dowson), Stribeck regime classification, and Hertz contact pressure — and cross-links tribological experiments to rheometry and impedance spectroscopy via shared lubricant IRI. A Random Forest model trained on GroupKFold cross-validation identifies Hertz peak pressure as the dominant predictor of CoF (SHAP importance ~35 %), ahead of coating composition and viscosity, providing a statistically grounded priority for experimental design.

5 Key Findings
1

Hertz pressure dominates CoF in lubricated DLC contacts

SHAP feature importance: ~35 % for Hertz peak pressure — higher than coating composition and lubricant viscosity combined. Contact geometry is the primary optimisation lever.

2

Automated FAIR integration across 8 measurement methods

B3P, GLEX, SRV, Galling, Abrasion (tribological) + Rheo, EIS, IR, Raman, DC_Conductivity (characterisation) — all producing 52+ physics-based Kennwerte per experiment with full PROV-O provenance.

3

Cross-modal linking: B3P <-> Rheology <-> EIS via shared lubricant IRI

HAS_COMPLEMENTARY_DATA edges connect tribological experiments to characterisation experiments of the same lubricant in the same project. SPARQL federation-ready.

4

First operational tribological PMDco-aligned OWL ontology (41 classes, v1.1)

All 6 Hartrott/MaterialDigital recommendations implemented: PMDco crosswalk, IAO textual definitions, SHACL 0 violations, named graphs per method, RepeatGroup nodes, OWL subclass hierarchy.

5

DPP data foundation: PFAS status, GWP, SVHC — SPARQL-queryable

Every lubricant carries regulatory metadata (PFAS-free/restricted/unknown, GWP in kg CO₂-eq/kg, SVHC flag). ESPR 2027 DPP data feed validated against PMDco v3.0 and SHACL shapes.

ta-C achieves Stribeck CoF minimum 0.005–0.025 under PAO lubrication

Factor 3–5x lower than uncoated 100Cr6 reference under identical conditions. The minimum occurs in the mixed/EHD regime where the DLC surface chemistry interaction with PAO is most effective.

12 Kennwerte automatically extracted per Stribeck curve

Boundary CoF, mixed-regime slope, hydrodynamic onset velocity, EHD film thickness (Hamrock-Dowson), Lambda ratio, and 7 additional regime-classification values — per experiment, automatically.

EHL film thickness: cross-modal Kennwert

h_c (Hamrock-Dowson 1977) computed from LSM surface roughness (Ra, Rq) + rheometry viscosity (eta at 40 °C, pressure-viscosity coefficient alpha). One Kennwert bridges three measurement methods.

Section B — Industrial & Regulatory Relevance
Circular Economy — GearOilLoop

28 B3P + 18 EIS measurements on identical gear oil samples across aging stages. DC resistance drops 37x under 42-day aging (46.6 to 1.25 GOhm) while B3P CoF shows non-monotonic response — EP additive activation at early aging stage. Cross-method KG enables data-driven recycled oil qualification.

PFAS-Free Lubricant Selection

Every lubricant in the knowledge graph carries PFAS status, GWP (kg CO2-eq/kg), and SVHC classification. A SPARQL query selects regulatory-compliant lubricants meeting tribological performance thresholds in under 1 second.

Digital Product Pass (ESPR 2027)

Machine-readable tribological data with PROV-O provenance traces — direct DPP data feed. Validated against PMDco v3.0 and SHACL shapes (0 violations). Implemented and operational, not planned.
